I’m Nobody! Who are you?

By Ave Jeanne Ventresca

I’m Nobody! Who are you?
Are you – Nobody – too?
Then there’s a pair of us!
Don’t tell! they’d advertise* – you know!

How dreary – to be – Somebody!
How public – like a Frog –
To tell your name – the livelong June –
To an admiring Bog!

Ave Jeanne Ventresca

I’m Ave Jeanne Ventresca (she/her), a white Italian American poet. Centered on social and environmental justice, my poetry explores the intersections of community, nature, and equity. I am the author of nine poetry chapbooks, including my latest collection, Noticing the Color of Ordinary, which received a Pushcart Prize nomination in 2019. For twenty years I worked as the publisher and editor of Black Bear Review, an international, award-winning small-press art and poetry literary magazine.
